Chương 1: Creativity Skills

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Nắm định nghĩa, cấp độ, thành phần, quy trình Wallas, đặc điểm người sáng tạo và các công cụ Mind Map / SWOT.
01

Khái niệm sáng tạo

Sáng tạo (Creativity) được trình bày như năng lực tạo ra ý tưởng mới và hữu ích, nhìn vấn đề bằng góc nhìn mới, tạo liên kết mới, học từ sai lầm và dùng trí tưởng tượng để mở ra khả năng khác. Ở cấp tổ chức, sáng tạo là việc cá nhân cùng làm việc trong một hệ thống xã hội phức tạp để tạo ra sản phẩm, dịch vụ, ý tưởng, quy trình hay thủ tục mới có giá trị.

Giải thích chi tiết
  • Spearman (1931): creativity is the process of the human mind creating new concepts by transforming relations and thereby generating new correlations.
  • Stagner & Karwoski (1973): creativity implies production of a totally or partially novel identity.
  • M.J. Levin (1978): creativity is the ability to discover new solutions to problems or produce new ideas, inventions, or works of art.
  • Creativity is the production of novel and useful ideas.
  • Creativity is the development of ideas about products, practices, services, or procedures that are novel and potentially useful to the organization.
  • Another definition emphasizes generating ideas/things valuable to the individual; looking at things with a fresh eye; examining problems with an open mind; making connections; learning from mistakes; and using imagination to explore new possibilities.
  • Skinner (1968): creative thinking means predictions and/or inferences that are new, original, and unusual for the individual; the creative thinker explores new areas and makes new observations, predictions, and inferences.
  • Organizational creativity: creation of a valuable, useful new product, service, idea, procedure, or process by individuals working together in a complex social system.
02

6 cấp độ sáng tạo

Sáu cấp độ đi từ nhớ kiến thức đến tạo ra cái mới: Remembering → Understanding → Applying → Analysing → Evaluating → Creating. Thứ tự 1–6 phải được giữ nguyên vì đây là cấu trúc của mô hình.

Giải thích chi tiết

Mô hình kim tự tháp có 6 tầng, từ dưới lên:

  1. Level 1 — Remembering: recall facts and basic concepts.
  2. Level 2 — Understanding: explain ideas or concepts.
  3. Level 3 — Applying: use information in new situations, to answer questions or solve problems.
  4. Level 4 — Analysing: take apart information to show relationships, causes, and connections.
  5. Level 5 — Evaluating: justify a stand or position.
  6. Level 6 — Creating: produce something new.
03

Componential Theory of Individual Creativity

Componential Theory of Individual Creativity gồm ba thành phần: Expertise (kiến thức cần thiết), Creative thinking (khả năng giải quyết vấn đề bằng tưởng tượng) và Task motivation (động lực bám theo vấn đề đến kết luận). Sơ đồ dùng nhãn Expertise / Motivation / Creative Thinking Skills và giao nhau tại Creativity.

Giải thích chi tiết

Componential Theory of Individual Creativity: creativity occurs when three components join together:

  • Expertise: necessary knowledge to put facts together.
  • Creative thinking: ability to imaginatively solve problems.
  • Task motivation: persevering/sticking with a problem to a conclusion.

Sơ đồ Venn ba vòng thể hiện Expertise, Motivation, and Creative Thinking Skills overlap; the central overlap is labeled Creativity.

04

Quy trình sáng tạo Graham Wallas (1926)

Graham Wallas (1926) mô tả 4 giai đoạn: Preparation → Incubation → Illumination → Verification. Chu trình này thuộc Chương 1 và không được trộn với mô hình 5 bước tạo ý tưởng ở Chương 6.

Giải thích chi tiết

Graham Wallas identifies four stages:

  1. Preparation: define the problem and gather as much information as possible about the subject; diagram label also describes focused attention and building the knowledge base.
  2. Incubation: let the mind process information subconsciously, allowing creative ideas to form without actively focusing on the problem; diagram shorthand: “let go and absorb.”
  3. Illumination: the “Eureka” / “Aha!” moment when a solution or idea comes unexpectedly, often during relaxation or distraction.
  4. Verification: test and refine the idea using critical thinking so that it works and is ready to be shared or implemented; diagram shorthand: craft the message and make it real.

Công cụ tư duy sáng tạo

Bốn nhóm công cụ tư duy sáng tạo được liệt kê: Brainstorming, Six Thinking Hats, Mind Mapping và SWOT. Mind map đi từ chủ đề trung tâm ra nhánh lớn, nhánh con, từ khóa và hình ảnh; SWOT nhìn theo Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats và kết hợp thành SO/WO/ST/WT.

Giải thích chi tiết

The chapter lists: Brainstorming; Six Thinking Hats; Mind Mapping Technique; SWOT Analysis Technique.

Mind Mapping Technique

  • Mind mapping represents information and relationships visually as a “mind map” / spider diagram.
  • Information is organized radially around a core concept.
  • A mind map starts with one central topic, branches outward into topics and subtopics, and may use images/icons.
  • Mind mapping can support better critical thinking and decision-making.

How to make a mind map:

  1. Define the central topic: put the focus of the map in the center.
  2. Create first-level topics: identify big categories related to the central topic and draw branches from the center.
  3. Expand branches: break first-level topics into subtopics like tree branches.
  4. Continue expanding as far as desired and try to use keywords for topics/subtopics.
  5. Add images if helpful: use small illustrations, doodles, images, or icons to personalize and make the content more memorable.

The worked visual uses “My To-Do List” as the center with first-level branches Home, Work, Study, Health, then progressively adds subtopics and images/icons.

SWOT Analysis Technique

  • SWOT is a strategic-planning technique using four lenses: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.
  • It helps identify ways the business can improve and maximize opportunities while identifying negative factors that may hinder success.
  • Applications listed: competitor analysis, situation analysis, strategic planning, personal evaluation, etc.

Chương 2: Brainstorm

Brainstorming: khái niệm & nguồn gốc

Brainstorming được Alex Osborn phát triển năm 1953. Đây là kỹ thuật tạo nhiều ý tưởng/giải pháp cho một vấn đề cụ thể dưới các nguyên tắc nhất định; có thể làm theo nhóm hoặc cá nhân. Nhóm lý tưởng trong khóa học là 5–7 người.

Giải thích chi tiết
  • Alex Osborn invented/developed brainstorming in 1953.
  • Brainstorming được mô tả như “một hội nghị sáng tạo để các nhóm người cùng đưa ra nhiều ý tưởng và tìm ra những cách tiếp cận mới cho vấn đề.”
  • It is a group-discussion technique for finding solutions to a specific problem by collecting participants’ ideas at the same time under certain principles.
  • It is a method to generate multiple creative solutions to a problem.
  • It no longer necessarily requires many people; one individual can carry it out.
  • The ideal brainstorming group is 5–7 participants.
02

Ưu và nhược điểm

Ưu điểm: mức tham gia cao, làm vấn đề sinh động, tạo dải ý tưởng rộng, tận dụng trí tuệ tập thể và tiết kiệm chi phí. Nhược điểm: tốn thời gian và dễ lan man/lệch chủ đề.

Giải thích chi tiết

Advantages: high participation; makes the problem more engaging/dynamic; encourages a wide range of ideas from many people; maximizes collective intelligence; cost-effective.

Disadvantages: time-consuming; may go off-topic or become scattered.

03

5 bước Brainstorming

Quy trình Brainstorming có đúng 5 bước: Preparation → Define the problem → Establish rules → Start brainstorming → Summarize ideas. Đánh giá chỉ bắt đầu sau khi phiên tạo ý tưởng kết thúc.

Giải thích chi tiết

Step 1 — Preparation: select a group leader/facilitator and a secretary to record all ideas; one person may perform both roles; tools may include A0 paper, markers, laptop, etc.

Step 2 — Define the business problem or situation.

Step 3 — Establish rules: leader has authority to manage/direct; members may not demand, interrupt, judge, criticize, or interfere with another member’s ideas/responses; no answer is considered wrong; collect all responses except duplicates; set a time limit and stop when time is up. Lưu ý: brainstorming explores a problem from multiple perspectives/points of view.

Step 4 — Start brainstorming: facilitator invites/designates members to share; secretary records responses and makes them visible if possible; ideas are expressed freely without restrictions; create a friendly/open atmosphere; encourage building upon others’ ideas; no evaluation/comments until the brainstorming session officially ends.

Step 5 — Summarize ideas: review all ideas and start evaluating; identify duplicate/overlapping ideas; group responses sharing similar principles/concepts; eliminate completely irrelevant ideas; after refining the list, discuss collective responses further.

Generative Thinking

Ý tưởng thường xuất hiện khi đầu óc/cơ thể thư giãn và có thể đến bất ngờ. Moore & Ditkoff (2008) nêu 5 chất xúc tác cho ý tưởng tốt: khi được truyền cảm hứng, brainstorm với người khác, chìm sâu trong dự án, khi vui vẻ và khi hợp tác với một partner.

Giải thích chi tiết
  • Ideas often come when brains and bodies are relaxed and when alone; they may happen when least expected.
  • Breakthrough ideas might come from a lot of ideas.

Top five catalysts for best ideas (Moore and Ditkoff, 2008):

  1. when you’re inspired;
  2. brainstorming with others;
  3. when you’re immersed in a project;
  4. when you’re happy;
  5. when you’re collaborating with a partner.
05

4 quy tắc Brainstorming

Bốn quy tắc: Strive for quantity; Seek the wild-and-unusual; Suspend judgement; Combine and build. Flow tham gia đi từ individual ideas → small groups → entire group.

Giải thích chi tiết
  1. Strive for quantity — maintain an “everything is possible” mindset.
  2. Seek the wild-and-unusual — remove the box and allow space for crazy/remarkable ideas.
  3. Suspend judgement — close down the analytical brain so ideas are not rejected too early and more ideas can flourish.
  4. Combine and build — improve ideas in different ways; normal or even bad ideas can become stepping stones to breakthroughs.

Flow of Brainstorming: individual ideas → small groups → entire group.

Ideation Toolkit

Ideation Toolkit bắt đầu từ một challenge được định nghĩa rõ, tạo càng nhiều ý tưởng càng tốt, dùng 5W1H và Six Thinking Hats, rồi thu được toàn bộ ý tưởng. 5W1H gồm What/Why/Where/Who/When/How; Six Hats gán sáu màu với sáu vai trò.

Giải thích chi tiết

The toolkit is shown as:

  • INPUT: clearly defined challenge.
  • PROCESS: generate as many ideas as possible.
  • TOOLS: 5W1H; Six Thinking Hats.
  • OUTPUT: all ideas.

5W1H Canvas

Purpose: understand the real problem from multiple standpoints by asking What, Why, Where, Who, When, How.

  • WHAT? Consider hard data (statistics, history, time factors) and soft data (opinions, human factors, attitudes, behaviors). Ask what has already been tried, what worked, what did not, and what outcome is desired.
  • WHY? Ask why repeatedly — at least five times — to reach root cause rather than symptom.
  • WHERE? Locate the best environment for action; where can the problem be resolved and help offered?
  • WHO? Who can help solve it? Who benefits? Identify direct and indirect participants.
  • WHEN? Determine deadline/timeframe to keep tasks on track.
  • HOW? How does the challenge influence people, activities, or events? Consider impacts on departments, resources, products, or tools.

DOs & DON’Ts khi Ideation

Checklist ideation nhấn mạnh tạo đúng môi trường, cởi mở, hoãn phán xét, nghĩ cá nhân rồi nhóm, ưu tiên số lượng, kết nối ý tưởng, ghi lại mọi ý tưởng, nghỉ giải lao và làm nghiêm túc nhưng vui. Ngược lại, không vội, không vừa tạo vừa đánh giá, không tiêu cực, không áp đảo, không đa nhiệm hay bác bỏ ý tưởng “điên”.

Giải thích chi tiết

DOs:

  • establish the correct environment;
  • keep an open mind;
  • suspend judgement;
  • brainstorm individually first, then in groups;
  • involve others;
  • focus on quantity;
  • give yourself time for thought experiments / focused daydreaming;
  • expand and build on ideas;
  • link seemingly random ideas — connect the unconnected;
  • capture all ideas;
  • take breaks;
  • listen to others;
  • use multiple small ideation sessions rather than one long meeting;
  • borrow another person’s point of reference;
  • look outside for inspiration — mirror others and improve;
  • sleep on it — allow incubation time;
  • ignore discouragement from doubters;
  • make it seriously fun.

DON’Ts:

  • rush idea generation;
  • create and evaluate at the same time;
  • be negative at this stage;
  • bully others with your ideas;
  • try and be sensible;
  • wander off topic;
  • multitask when generating ideas;
  • dismiss insane ideas as impossible;
  • keep an “infeasible mindset”;
  • fall foul of “listening to the experts” syndrome;
  • lack confidence;
  • brainstorm without structure.

Chương 3: Critical Thinking

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Phân biệt 6 levels, 2 types, 6 bước luyện, vai trò và cách dùng ARES cùng 9 thinking standards theo đúng thuật ngữ khóa học.
01

Critical Thinking: định nghĩa & giá trị

Critical Thinking là quá trình phân tích, đánh giá và đặt câu hỏi với giả định để hình thành quan điểm, chứng minh/bảo vệ quan điểm logic và phản bác quan điểm đối lập. Người có tư duy phản biện không chỉ nhìn bề mặt mà đào sâu lập luận, nhìn nhiều góc và chủ động học/làm.

Giải thích chi tiết

Critical thinking is presented as the process of analyzing, evaluating, and questioning assumptions/suppositions so a person can form a way of thinking, give an opinion on an issue, prove and defend opinions logically/consistently, and refute opposing opinions.

People with critical thinking: do not accept problems only at the surface; dig deeper into related arguments/issues; see the world from different perspectives; adapt to change; handle situations more easily; and should actively own this skill for study/work instead of passively receiving information.

02

6 cấp độ Critical Thinking

Khóa học chia tư duy phản biện thành 6 mức: Unreflective → Challenged → Beginning → Practicing → Advanced → Master. Mỗi mức đi từ diễn đạt rõ vấn đề đến lập luận có cấu trúc, luyện tập thường xuyên, nhìn ra giả định và đạt các phẩm chất như công bằng, chính trực, dũng cảm, khiêm tốn, bền bỉ và đồng cảm.

Giải thích chi tiết
  1. Level 1 — Unreflective thinkers: failure to state content clearly makes meetings/discussions long and unresolved; state specific content clearly from the start.
  2. Level 2 — Challenged thinkers: structured expression helps listeners understand without excessive explanation; suggested opening: “My opinion on this issue is…”
  3. Level 3 — Beginning thinkers: arguments may come from two or more perspectives to refute the initial opinion; use scientific arguments/authentic evidence and be willing to accept others’ positive opinions.
  4. Level 4 — Practicing thinkers: regular practice supports more logical evaluation of opinions/problems.
  5. Level 5 — Advanced thinkers: debate becomes positive/constructive when implicit assumptions behind opposing opinions are identified; consistency and logic are required.
  6. Level 6 — Master thinkers: critical thinking meets elements of fairness, integrity, courage, humility, persistence, and sympathy.
03

2 loại Critical Thinking theo khóa học

Hai loại: Convergent critical thinking (Tư duy phản biện tự điều chỉnh) và Divergent critical thinking (Tư duy phản biện ngoại cảnh). Convergent đi đến đáp án tốt nhất dựa trên trí nhớ, tài nguyên quanh mình hoặc logic; Divergent đưa ý kiến của mình để phê bình/đối chiếu các ý kiến khác trong cùng vấn đề.

Giải thích chi tiết

The course labels two types: Convergent critical thinking (Tư duy phản biện tự điều chỉnh) and Divergent critical thinking (Tư duy phản biện ngoại cảnh).

Convergent critical thinking: process of arriving at the best answer using memory, surrounding resources, or logic; a convergent thinker applies established procedures and memory recall to reach the “correct” answer.

People described under this type are calm, thoughtful, rational, and rarely make hasty conclusions.

Divergent critical thinking: giving one’s own opinion to criticize other people’s opinions on the same issue; common in teamwork where different solutions are analyzed/criticized together to reach a common conclusion.

Conflicting thoughts/opinions can make the problem more diverse/objective and support more comprehensive/appropriate solutions.

Vai trò của Critical Thinking

Bốn vai trò được liệt kê: quan trọng cho nền kinh tế; thúc đẩy sáng tạo; cải thiện kỹ năng trình bày và năng lực ngôn ngữ; hỗ trợ self-reflection để đánh giá lối sống, quan điểm và chính mình.

Giải thích chi tiết
  • Important for the economy: future dependence on technology, information, innovation means critical thinking is essential for developing economies to solve problems quickly/effectively.
  • Promote creativity: supports rapid problem solving and innovative/creative ideas, then analysis/adaptation of those ideas in reality.
  • Improve presentation skills and language capacities: clear/systematic thinking makes expression easier and improves analysis/thinking/understanding.
  • Self-reflection: provides tools to justify/evaluate lifestyle and viewpoints and to evaluate oneself.

6 bước luyện Critical Thinking

Sáu bước luyện critical thinking: Identify problem/question → Gather data/opinions/arguments → Analyze & evaluate data → Think reversely and ask questions → Evaluation → Reach a conclusion.

Giải thích chi tiết
  1. Identify the problem/question: ask what you know, how you know it, and what you aim to prove/disprove/express.
  2. Gather data, opinions, arguments: research deeply; collect multidimensional sources including materials contradicting your own opinion.
  3. Analyze and evaluate data: check trustworthiness, remove prejudiced/bias opinions, select views supported by convincing evidence.
  4. Think reversely and ask questions: invert complex problems; example: “A caused B, but what happens if B causes A?”
  5. Evaluation: ask who collected the information, how they found it, and why; ask yourself, “Am I favored of any side?”
  6. Reach a conclusion: identify possible conclusions, compare strengths/limitations, then share the final conclusion with different audience groups.

Giải thích chi tiết

  • Activities: goal-directed sets of actions/pathways toward things people want to accomplish; ask what activities/actions people engage in while carrying out tasks.

  • Objects: physical items/building blocks people interact with; identify objects/devices used and how they relate to activities.
  • Users: people whose behaviors, needs, preferences are being observed; ask about goals, values, motivations, roles, prejudices, relationships, and who they are.

Persuasion & Speech Acts

Persuasion là hành vi giao tiếp vừa hướng tới được hiểu vừa được tin. Theo Austin (1962), ba speech acts được minh họa: locutionary, illocutionary và perlocutionary bằng ví dụ Coca-Cola.

Giải thích chi tiết

Marie-Odile Taillard is quoted: communication aims to be understood and believed; persuasion is the communicative act carrying out both goals. Bài học nhấn mạnh rằng persuasion constitutes a “speech act,” an act performed in or by speaking.

Austin’s theory of language (1962):

  • Locutionary act: a young woman holds up Coca-Cola and says “Coke is the real thing.”
  • Illocutionary act: by saying it, she asserts that Coke is “the real thing.”
  • Perlocutionary act: the utterance persuades viewers that drinking Coke is a worthwhile experience.

A Dale Carnegie quotation used in the chapter states that the way to get someone to do something is by giving them what they want; câu hỏi gợi mở là “What do you want?”

Logos / Ethos / Pathos

Ba nguyên tắc theo cách khóa học trình bày: Logos = bằng chứng và logic nhất quán; Ethos = chuẩn mực đạo đức chung như công lý/công bằng/trách nhiệm; Pathos = cảm xúc kỳ vọng từ audience và khả năng đặt mình vào trạng thái cảm xúc của người khác.

Giải thích chi tiết

  • Ethos: commonality of the speaker’s and listener’s moral norms, including justice, fairness, responsibility.
  • Pathos: feelings expected from the audience; balance between speaker/listener emotions; ability to put oneself in another person’s emotional state, described as empathy.

Giải thích chi tiết
  1. Observing Trends
  2. Solving a Problem
  3. Finding Gaps in the Marketplace

Approach 1 — Observing Trends

Important trends: economic forces; social forces; technological advances; political action and regulatory change. Entrepreneurs should stay aware of changes in these areas.

Environmental-trend diagram:

  • Economic forces: state of the economy; level of disposable income; consumer spending patterns.
  • Social forces: social and cultural trends; demographic changes; what people think is “in.”
  • Technological advances: new technologies; emerging technologies; new uses of old technologies.
  • Political and regulatory changes: new changes in the political arena; new laws and regulations.
  • These feed into a business/product/service opportunity gap — the difference between what’s available and what’s possible — which can lead to new business/product/service ideas.

Economic forces: help identify areas ripe for startups and areas to avoid. Example: weak economy favors startups helping consumers save money; GasBuddy.com is cited as helping save money on gas.

Social forces: alter behavior/priorities and open opportunities. Examples: aging baby boomers; increasing workplace diversity; interest in Facebook/Twitter social networks; focus on health/wellness; interest in green products.

Technological advances: frequently create opportunities. Industries/examples listed: computer industry; Internet; biotechnology; digital photography.

Once technology is created, products can emerge to advance it. H2OAudio is described as a company started by four former San Diego State University students making waterproof housings for Apple iPhone/iPod.

Political action/regulatory changes: also create opportunity; environmental laws can create firms helping other firms comply.

Ví dụ: a company created to help others comply with a specific law; specific example listed: Internet security law.

Approach 2 — Solving a Problem

Identifying opportunities can involve noticing a problem and finding a way to solve it. Problems may be identified via trends or through intuition, serendipity, or chance.

Example: finding alternatives to fossil fuels; entrepreneurial firms such as solar farms are launched to solve this problem.

Approach 3 — Finding Gaps in the Marketplace

A gap exists when a product/service is needed by a specific group but the market is not large enough to interest mainstream retailers/manufacturers.

Example: in 2000 Tish Cirovolv is described as noticing no guitars designed specifically for women and starting Daisy Rock Guitars to fill the gap.

Yếu tố cá nhân hỗ trợ Opportunity Recognition

Kỹ thuật tạo & bảo vệ Ideas

Kỹ thuật tạo ideas gồm Brainstorming, Focus Groups và Library/Internet Research; thêm Customer Advisory Boards, idea bank/vault và 3 bước bảo vệ idea: đưa idea vào dạng hữu hình và ghi ngày, bảo mật, tránh disclosure làm mất quyền độc quyền.

Giải thích chi tiết

Overview: Brainstorming; Focus Groups; Library and Internet Research.

Brainstorming: generate many ideas/solutions quickly; session typically involves a group and targets a specific topic. Rules: no criticism; freewheeling encouraged; move quickly; leap-frogging encouraged.

Focus Group: 5–10 people selected by common characteristics related to the issue; led by a trained moderator who uses group dynamics to understand why participants feel as they do; can generate business ideas.

Library Research: an underutilized resource for ideas; reference librarians can point to industry-specific magazines, trade journals, industry reports; browsing these can spark ideas.

Large public/university libraries may provide expensive resources. Examples: BizMiner, ProQuest, IBISWorld, Mintel, LexisNexis Academic.

Internet Research: search “new business ideas” for articles; set up Google/Yahoo email alerts for a topic; targeted searches are useful.

Customer Advisory Boards: companies may create boards meeting regularly to discuss needs, wants, problems that may lead to ideas.

Encouraging New Ideas: appoint a specific person to screen/track ideas; or create an idea bank/vault, physical or digital repository for ideas.

Protecting Ideas From Being Lost or Stolen:

  1. Put the idea in tangible form (physical logbook/computer disk) and record the date first thought of.
  2. Secure the idea.
  3. Avoid inadvertent/voluntary disclosure that forfeits rights to claim exclusivity.

Entrepreneurship & Entrepreneur

Ch.7 tiếp tục định nghĩa entrepreneurship với nhấn mạnh tạo value, nhận diện business opportunity, quản lý risk và huy động nguồn lực; đồng thời mô tả là quá trình starting and operating your own business. Các ví dụ Ford, Hewlett/Packard được dùng để minh họa trong bài.

Giải thích chi tiết

One definition: entrepreneurship is the attempt to create value through recognition of business opportunity, management of risk taking, and communication/management skills to mobilize human, financial, material resources needed to bring a project to fruition.

It can also be understood as one or more people starting to build their own business model — creating and growing a new business. Entrepreneurs connect creative ideas with purposeful action and business structure.

Một định nghĩa khác: entrepreneurship là quá trình starting and operating your own business and entrepreneurs as people who create, launch, organize, manage a new business and take its risk.

Examples given: Henry Ford introduced mass vehicle production to make vehicles affordable to average people; William Hewlett and David Packard started a small business in Packard’s garage; ví dụ nêu rằng họ đã phát minh floppy disks and the first pocket calculator.

Business Model Canvas — 9 blocks

Business Model Canvas (BMC) là biểu diễn trực quan gồm đúng 9 building blocks. Chín block: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Revenue Streams, Customer Relationships, Key Activities, Key Resources, Key Partners, Cost Structure.

Giải thích chi tiết

Definition: a visual representation of 9 key building blocks forming foundations of a business; a khung trực quan for systematic invention/design/building of models; useful for startups and annual planning.

The nine blocks as explained in the chapter:

  1. Customer Segments: who is the customer? Group key customers by pain points, demographics, buyer behavior, or relevant characteristics.
  2. Value Propositions: what makes you better than others? Solve customer problems/satisfy needs with value propositions.
  3. Channels: how communicate the value proposition? Marketing/distribution channels, including specific social media, paid ads, print, etc.
  4. Revenue Streams: how will you make money? Consider wholesale, e-commerce, physical store, add-on services; primary/secondary/additional sources.
  5. Customer Relationships: how interact with customers? Think through sales process and lead journey.
  6. Key Activities: activities enabling delivery of value proposition.
  7. Key Resources: assets/resources owned or controlled that support success.
  8. Key Partners: advisors, mentors, supporting organizations, chamber of commerce, lawyer, accountant.
  9. Cost Structure: fixed/variable costs; major costs such as R&D, capital expenditures, cost of goods sold.

Hai cách bố trí BMC cùng sử dụng chín thành phần. Một cách nhóm các thành phần quanh Value Proposition và phân biệt “things that cost money,” “the mission,” và “things that make money.”
