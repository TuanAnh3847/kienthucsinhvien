Chương 1: Accounting for Main Business Transactions in Enterprises

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Nắm luồng kế toán từ nguyên vật liệu, tài sản cố định và tiền lương đến chi phí sản xuất, thành phẩm, doanh thu và các khoản giảm trừ doanh thu.

1A · Inventory & Tools/Supplies

Materials (Nguyên vật liệu)

152
  • Inputs used in production, purchased externally or produced by the enterprise.
  • Main materials; auxiliary materials; fuel; replacement supplies; construction materials.

Supplies / Tools

153
  • Means of production that do not meet fixed-asset recognition thresholds.
  • Supplies; packaging; tools for rental; tools, molds and spare parts.

Receipt measurement

Cost
Initially measured at cost.

Reporting date

Lower of
Measured at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV).

NRV

Definition
Estimated selling price less estimated completion and selling costs.

Các phương pháp tính giá hàng tồn kho

Scope
  • Specific identification
  • Weighted-average cost: moving weighted average or periodic weighted average
  • First-in, first-out (FIFO)

Chương 3: Accounting for Receivables and Payables / Payments

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Hiểu cách 131/331 có thể mang số dư hai phía; theo dõi 2293, VAT 133/3331, current CIT 8211/3334 và borrowings 3411 mà không net sai số dư.

Receivables & payables — the dual-balance warning

Account 131 — Accounts receivable

Do not net
  • Dr: increase trade receivable.
  • Cr: collections and customer advances.
  • Debit subsidiary balance = receivable asset.
  • Credit subsidiary balance = customer advance liability.

Account 331 — Accounts payable

Do not net
  • Cr: increase accounts payable.
  • Dr: payments and advances to suppliers.
  • Debit subsidiary balance = supplier prepayment asset.
  • Credit subsidiary balance = payable liability.

Each receivable/payable is tracked by counterparty, maturity, nature, original currency and other management information. Current/non-current classification uses remaining term; debit and credit balances are presented separately from subsidiary-ledger balances.

131 / 331 Balance Splitter

Enter subsidiary balances — never net debit and credit

Bad-debt lifecycle — 2293

Allowance is recognized when receivables are overdue with unsuccessful collection efforts, or not yet due but the debtor is bankrupt, in dissolution, missing or absconded. The amount is estimated theo quy định áp dụng cho tình huống.

VAT — 133 and 3331 stay distinct

133 — Deductible VAT

Input VAT
  • Dr: input VAT.
  • Cr: deduction, non-deductible VAT transfer, VAT refund.
  • Non-deductible input VAT is included in asset cost, COGS or operating expenses as appropriate.

3331 — VAT payable

Output VAT
  • Cr: output VAT payable.
  • Dr: input VAT deduction and VAT adjustments on returns/allowances.
  • Output VAT is collected on behalf of the State and excluded from revenue.

Các mức dùng trong ví dụ: 0%, 5%, 10%. Các mức thuế áp dụng trong ví dụ.
