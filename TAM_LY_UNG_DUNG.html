Psychology là gì?

Psychology là scientific study of the mind and behavior, quan tâm cách con người nghĩ, cảm nhận và hành động ở cấp độ cá nhân/nhóm.

Psychology: the scientific study of the mind and behavior.

Các mối quan tâm gồm perception, emotion, learning, memory, personality, social interactions và underlying biological processes; định hướng chung còn nhắc đến understanding/explaining, mental health, personal development và well-being.

Experimental

Manipulate an independent variable to determine its effect on a dependent variable while controlling extraneous factors. Example: sleep deprivation → cognitive performance.

Correlational

Examine relationships between two or more variables without manipulation. Example: stress level ↔ academic performance.

Observation

Naturalistic: natural environment, e.g. playground behavior. Controlled: structured setting, e.g. laboratory.

Case study

In-depth analysis of an individual, group or event; Ví dụ: rare psychological disorder in one person.

Survey / questionnaire

Structured self-report data from a large group; Ví dụ: public opinions on mental-health stigma.

Interview

Detailed direct face-to-face/virtual conversation; structured = pre-set questions; unstructured = open-ended/flexible discussion.

Longitudinal

Same participants over extended time; Ví dụ: cognitive development childhood → adulthood.

Cross-sectional

Different groups at one point; Ví dụ: stress levels among teenagers, adults and seniors.

Thresholds & Weber’s Law

Absolute threshold

Smallest stimulus intensity that can be perceived; differs by individual; lower threshold = greater sensitivity.

Noise

Stimuli interfering with perception of other stimuli. Example: bad smell while eating.

Signal detection & sensory adaptation

Signal detection theory

Weak-stimulus discrimination depends on physiological sensitivity plus personality traits, expectations, alertness, motivation and prejudices.

Ví dụ y khoa minh họa: doctor distinguishes normal vs illness-associated heartbeat; expectations, beliefs, knowledge, treatment experience and motivation can affect detection.

Two stated observer errors: report stimulus when absent; report no stimulus when present.

Sensory adaptation

Adjustment after long exposure to the same stimulus; repeated/long exposure can reduce noticing/responding.

Ngoại lệ: no adaptation to extremely high intensities, especially painful stimuli (severe toothache, extremely loud noise).

Mental set

Tendency to approach a problem in a particular way; a method that worked before may not be effective now.
