Money vs Wealth vs Income

Money

Bất cứ thứ gì được chấp nhận rộng rãi để thanh toán hàng hóa/dịch vụ hoặc hoàn trả nợ. Money là một stock concept.

Wealth

Tổng bộ sưu tập tài sản dùng để lưu giữ giá trị; ví dụ: stocks, bonds, land. Đây không đồng nghĩa với money.

Income

Dòng thu nhập theo một đơn vị thời gian — một flow, trái với money được trình bày như stock.

06 · REGULATION & LIMITS

Regulation, intermediary functions, locked syllabus nodes

Regulation aims

  • Increase information available to investors.
  • Reduce adverse selection / moral hazard and insider trading.
  • Ensure soundness via chartering/entry restrictions, disclosure, asset/activity restrictions, deposit insurance; bài học also notes historical limits on competition.

Intermediary benefits

  • Lower transaction costs via economies of scale.
  • Provide liquidity services.
  • Reduce risk exposure.
  • Risk sharing / asset transformation / diversification.

Chương 4: Theory of Asset Demand

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Ghi nhớ bốn yếu tố ảnh hưởng asset demand, áp dụng đúng expected-return phương pháp đã học và hiểu vai trò của information, expectations và market relationships.
01 · FOUR FACTORS

Theory of Asset Demand

Bốn yếu tố theo bài học và hướng tác động lên demand của asset.

Wealth ↑Demand ↑

Wealth = all economic resources. Necessity asset elasticity <1; luxury asset elasticity >1.

Expected Return ↑Demand ↑

Higher expected return raises demand for that asset, other things unchanged.

Relative Risk ↑Demand ↓

Most investors are described as risk averse.

Relative Liquidity ↑Demand ↑

Liquidity = convert to cash easily at low fee.

Risk & diversification — cách trình bày trong bài

Diversification can reduce portfolio risk when risky assets have opposite expected-return trends. The bài học names diversification risk and non-diversification risk, and states higher non-diversification risk is associated with higher return.

01 · INTEREST RATE

Định nghĩa và classifications

Real rate

02 · CREDIT INSTRUMENTS

4 instruments trong credit market

Simple loan

Principal + interest at maturity.

100m · 1 year · 10% → bài học repayment 110m.

Fixed repayment loan

Fixed amount at fixed intervals.

1 billion · 15 years · 150m each year.

Coupon bond

Periodic coupon until maturity, then face value.

Face 100m · coupon 10%/year · annual.

Discount / zero-coupon bond

Sold below face; no coupon; face paid at maturity.

Face 10m · price 9m · 1 year.
Simple interest

Calculated on principal only; prior interest is not added to principal. Bài học states use for contracts with maturity ≤1 year and proportional annual-rate conversion for fractions of a year.

Compound interest

Accrued interest is added to principal for the next period; bài học states it is usually used for long-term contracts >1 year. Execution remains blocked under typography ambiguity.

Movement vs shift

Price/rate changes cause movement along curves. Non-price/non-rate factors shift curves.

Chương 6: The Basics of Exchange Rates

Mục tiêu trọng tâm: Nắm định nghĩa/quotation methods, appreciation-depreciation, Law of One Price, PPP, các factor groups và exchange-rate systems trong phạm vi bài.
01 · FX BASICS

Foreign Exchange Rate & transaction categories

FX

Foreign exchange rate

Value of one currency in terms of another.

Standardized vs open-market (OTC)Open vs closeSpot vs forward
Appreciation

Currency increases in value. Bài học implication: domestic goods/services become more expensive and foreign goods cheaper; bài học frames this as benefiting imports.

Depreciation

Reverse of appreciation; bài học states depreciation is better for exports.

02 · QUOTATION

Commodity currency, term currency, direct & indirect quotation

Commodity currency

Currency whose value is expressed in another currency.

Term currency

Currency used to define another currency’s value.

Direct quotation

Domestic currency = term currency; foreign currency = commodity currency.

Indirect quotation

Foreign currency = term currency; domestic currency = commodity currency.

Nhận định trong bài: most countries use direct quotation except USD, GBP, AUD, EUR. This is preserved as cách diễn đạt trong bài and not externally updated.

03 · LONG-RUN

Law of One Price, PPP & long-term factors

Law of One Price

Một security, commodity hoặc asset có cùng giá ở mọi nơi khi exchange rates được xét.

Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)

Hai currency ở equilibrium/par khi một basket of goods, xét exchange rate, có cùng mức giá ở hai quốc gia.

General long-term principle

Factors that increase demand for domestic goods relative to foreign goods cause domestic currency appreciation, and vice versa.

COMPARISON HUB

20 cặp / nhóm dễ nhầm

1. Money vs Wealth vs Income

Money = generally accepted payment & stock concept; Wealth = property storing value; Income = flow per unit time.

2. Commodity vs Fiat Money

Commodity has alternative economic use; Fiat is by governmental decree/legal tender without convertibility.

3. Four functions of money

Medium of exchange · Unit of account · Store of value · Standard of deferred payment.

4. M1 vs M2

M1 links to medium of exchange; M2 adds bài học-listed store-of-value components.

5. SSU vs DSU

SSU: income > spending. DSU: spending > income.

6. Direct vs Indirect Finance

Direct: SSU↔DSU; Indirect: intermediary transforms claims between them.

7. Debt vs Equity

Debt = borrowing/lending claim; Equity = ownership claim.

8. Primary vs Secondary

Primary = new issue; Secondary = previously issued securities.

9. Exchange vs OTC

Exchange = central location; OTC = dealers in different locations.

10. Money vs Capital Market

Money = short-term debt <1 year; Capital = longer-term debt/equity >1 year.

11. Broker vs Dealer

Broker transacts for clients; Dealer carries inventory/makes markets and earns bid–ask spread.

12. Bond vs Stock

Bond = borrowing / future interest+face; Stock = ownership / dividends+sale price.

13. Common vs Preferred Stock

Common = residual ownership, variable dividends; Preferred = fixed dividend/preference, creditor-like protection.

14. Bank Loan vs Bond vs Stock

Use exact 8-dimension table in Ch.2.

15. Adverse Selection vs Moral Hazard

Before transaction vs after transaction.

16. Intermediary categories

Depository · Contractual savings · Investment intermediaries.

17. Simple vs Compound Interest

Simple = interest on principal only; Compound = accrued interest added to principal, nhưng calculator compound vẫn bị khóa do bài học typography chưa verify.

18. Nominal vs Real vs Effective

Nominal unadjusted; Real adjusted for expected inflation; Effective là một bài học term riêng; wording của bài học còn ambiguity nên không đồng nhất với real rate.

19. Loanable Funds vs Liquidity Preference

Bond demand/supply vs money demand/supply determine equilibrium interest rate.

20. Direct vs Indirect FX quotation

Direct: domestic currency term; Indirect: foreign currency term.
